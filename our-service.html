Our service

Professional pathway research, tailored to you

This is a research service. We look at your starting point and your destination, research the routes that apply to you, and explain what we find clearly.

This isn't career counselling.

We don't tell you what career you should choose. You choose the destination. We research the routes.

What we research

The route between where you are and where you want to go

Qualifications

The qualifications that lead toward your destination.

Institutions

Public and private institutions that offer them.

Published entry requirements

APS, required subjects and minimum marks.

How your marks compare

Your current marks set against those requirements, with gaps made clear.

Direct routes

Where you can go straight into the qualification you need.

Documented alternative routes

Other ways in, included only when an authoritative source confirms them.

Progression pathways

Documented progression from one qualification to the next.

Costs and applications

Fees, funding and application considerations, where relevant and reliable.

Sources and academic year

Where each finding comes from, and which year it applies to.

What you receive

Your Pathway Report

A personalised report that brings the research together, so you and your family can compare the routes and decide what to do next. Where relevant, it covers:

  • Your goal or destination
  • Your current position
  • Relevant qualifications and institutions
  • Entry requirements
  • How your marks or position compare
  • Direct and alternative routes
  • Progression options
  • Costs and application considerations
  • Sources
  • Clear next steps
