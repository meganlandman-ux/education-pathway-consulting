FAQs

Questions people ask us

Short answers to common questions. If yours isn't here, ask us directly.

Do you tell me what career I should choose?

No. You choose the destination, and we research the education routes that could get you there.

Do I need to know exactly what I want to study?

No. A career, profession or type of work is enough. Finding the relevant qualifications is part of the research.

What will I actually receive?

A personalised Pathway Report, with sources and next steps. See what it covers.

Will everyone with the same goal get the same pathway?

No. Your route depends on where you are starting from. See what shapes a pathway.

Why do you ask for my marks and circumstances?

We compare your marks with published entry requirements, and use your location, budget and preferences to focus on routes that are realistic for you.

Do you only research universities?

No. Depending on your destination and circumstances, we may research universities, private higher education institutions, TVET colleges and other relevant education pathways.

What if I don't meet the requirements?

That's where pathway research is most useful. We look for documented alternative routes where they exist.

Do you guarantee admission?

No. We research published requirements and documented pathways. Meeting published minimum requirements doesn't guarantee a place, especially for competitive programmes. Final admission decisions rest with each institution.

Do you work with parents?

Yes. Parents and guardians can complete the process with or on behalf of a learner.

Ready to map your road?

You choose the destination. We map the road.
