The problem

Not sure how to get from where you are to where you want to be?

Knowing what you want is a big step. Working out how to get there is a different job, and the education system doesn't make it easy.

There are universities, universities of technology, private colleges and TVET colleges. Each has its own qualifications, entry requirements, application dates and costs. The information is spread across dozens of websites and prospectuses, and it changes every year.

Education Pathway Consulting researches these questions for you and turns the answers into one clear, personalised pathway.

What makes this different

We don't just tell you what you could study. We research how you could get there.

Career guidance helps people decide what to become. We start after that decision. You bring the destination, and we research the education routes between your current position and that goal, using published requirements and documented pathways.

General career guidance

  • Helps you explore what you could study or become
  • Often starts with interests, aptitudes or personality
  • Gives general information about options

Education pathway research

  • Starts with the destination you've chosen
  • Compares your marks and subjects with published requirements
  • Looks for documented alternative routes when the direct route isn't open yet
  • Shows the sources behind the findings
How it works

From "I want to…" to a mapped route

You tell us where you want to go. We research the possible education routes, map the road from where you are now, and give you a clear, evidence-based pathway with next steps.

  1. 01
    Intake form

    You tell us where you want to go

    Complete a short intake form about your destination, where you are now academically, and the circumstances that affect your options.

  2. 02
    Route research

    We research the possible routes

    We investigate the qualifications, institutions, published entry requirements, alternative routes and costs that are relevant to your situation.

  3. 03
    Pathway map

    We map the pathway from your current position

    We compare where you are now with what each route requires, and map the possible routes from your starting point to your destination.

  4. 04
    Pathway + next steps

    You receive a clear pathway and next steps

    You get your pathway report and a chance to talk through the findings, your options and what to do next.

Why every pathway is different

Two people with the same destination can have very different routes. Your research is shaped by:

  • Your destination
  • Current academic position
  • Subjects and marks
  • Location
  • Budget
  • Study preferences
  • Other circumstances

What you receive

Your Pathway Report

A personalised report setting out the education routes between where you are now and where you want to go. Depending on your circumstances and the service you choose, your pathway research can include:

Qualifications

The qualification or qualifications that lead toward your destination.

Institutions

Public and private institutions that offer those qualifications.

Entry requirements

Published admission requirements, such as APS, required subjects and minimum marks.

How your marks compare

Your current subjects and marks set against those requirements, with any gaps made clear.

Alternative routes

Routes to consider when the direct route isn't currently open to you.

Progression pathways

Documented progression from one qualification to the next, where it exists.

Costs and applications

Relevant fees, funding and application considerations, where reliable information is available.

Sources

References for the research, so you can see where each finding comes from.

Clear next steps

Practical actions to take from where you are now.

We don't guarantee admission. Meeting published minimum requirements doesn't always mean you'll be accepted, especially for competitive programmes. We tell you clearly when selection applies.

FAQs

Questions people ask us

Can't find your answer? Ask us a question.

Do you tell me what career I should choose?

No. You tell us what you want to do or be. We research the education pathways that could help you get there.

Do I need to know exactly what I want to study?

No. You can tell us your desired career, profession or type of work, and we can research the qualification routes associated with it.

What will I actually receive?

A personalised pathway report. Depending on your circumstances and the service you choose, it can cover the relevant qualifications and institutions, published entry requirements, how your marks compare, direct and alternative routes, progression, costs and application considerations, sources, and clear next steps. We also talk you through the findings.

Will everyone with the same goal get the same pathway?

No. Your pathway depends on where you are starting from: your current academic position, subjects and marks, location, budget, study preferences and other circumstances. Two people who want to become nurses may have very different routes.

Why do you ask for my marks and circumstances?

Because that is what we research. We compare your marks and subjects with published requirements, and use your location, budget and preferences to focus on routes that are realistic for you.

Do you only research universities?

No. Depending on the destination and your circumstances, we may research universities, private higher education institutions, TVET colleges and other relevant education pathways.

What if I don't meet the requirements?

That is exactly where pathway research can be useful. We investigate documented alternative routes where they exist.

Do you guarantee admission?

No. We research published requirements and documented pathways. Final admission decisions remain with the relevant institution.

Do you work with parents?

Yes. Parents and guardians can complete the process with or on behalf of a learner.
