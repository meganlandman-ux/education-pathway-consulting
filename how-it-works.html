How it works

From "I want to…" to a mapped route

You tell us where you want to go. We research the possible education routes and map the road from where you are now.

The four steps

  1. 01
    Intake form

    You tell us where you want to go

    Complete a short intake form about your destination, where you are now academically, and the circumstances that affect your options.

  2. 02
    Route research

    We research the possible routes

    We investigate the qualifications, institutions, published entry requirements, alternative routes and costs that are relevant to your situation.

  3. 03
    Pathway map

    We map the pathway from your current position

    We compare where you are now with what each route requires, and map the possible routes from your starting point to your destination.

  4. 04
    Pathway + next steps

    You receive a clear pathway and next steps

    You get your pathway report and a chance to talk through the findings, your options and what to do next.

Why every pathway is different

Two people with the same destination can have very different routes. Your research is shaped by:

  • Your destination
  • Current academic position
  • Subjects and marks
  • Location
  • Budget
  • Study preferences
  • Other circumstances
